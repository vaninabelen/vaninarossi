About

Education Specialist with a BA in Communication. I've been working at the intersection of education, technology, and communication for over 10 years.

What defines me is the ability to connect worlds. I believe learning is a collective process, and that designing good learning experiences can transform teams, organizations, and communities. I'm especially interested in how we structure knowledge in the age of generative AI — so that learning remains human. That's what drives me.

At Mercado Libre, I designed knowledge management strategies for IT & Product and currently lead the AI strategy for company-wide learning experiences. Before that, I worked at Chicos.net developing educational content on digital citizenship and AI. From there, projects reached students and teachers in Uruguay, Chile, Mexico, and Argentina — through partnerships with Disney (Historias para Armar) and Plan Ceibal.

I was also a Digital Pedagogical Advisor in public schools in Buenos Aires for five years, and started my career as a classroom teacher through Enseñá por Argentina (Teach For All).
